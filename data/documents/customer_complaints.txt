Spoonful Customer Complaint Handling. Every complaint is logged in the CRM and acknowledged within 4 business hours, with a named owner and ticket reference. Severity P1 means a core feature is unusable or data is at risk, with a 24 hour resolution target and immediate escalation to the on-call support lead. P2 means significant degradation with a workaround, resolved within 3 business days. P3 covers minor issues, resolved within 7 business days. Owners update customers daily for P1 and every two business days for P2. Support agents can approve refunds or credits up to $200, team leads up to $1,000, and larger amounts need the Director of Customer Success. Within 5 business days of closing, the customer gets a satisfaction survey, and scores of 6 or below trigger a call from the team lead. P1 and repeat complaints are reviewed monthly with Product and Engineering to find root causes.
