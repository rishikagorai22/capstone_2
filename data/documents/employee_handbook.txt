Spoonful Employee Handbook: Satisfaction and Wellbeing. An anonymous satisfaction survey runs twice a year, in March and September, scored 1 to 10. The company target is an average of 7.5 or higher in every department. The People team's internal benchmark for comparable technology companies is a median of 7.2. Spoonful is hybrid, with two office days per week. Employees get 25 days of paid leave, 4 wellbeing days per year, and 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 dollars per year. Managers hold weekly one-to-one meetings. Promotion cycles run in April and October. On-call staff receive on-call pay and a rest day after night-time pages. Concerns can be raised with a manager, the People team, or anonymously through the ethics line.
