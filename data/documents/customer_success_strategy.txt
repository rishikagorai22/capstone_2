Spoonful Customer Success Strategy. New customers follow a 30-60-90 day onboarding plan: setup and training in the first 30 days, regular use of at least two core features by day 60, and a value review at day 90. Every account has a health score from 0 to 100, made of product usage (40 percent), support experience (20 percent), customer satisfaction or NPS (20 percent), and billing history (20 percent). Accounts below 60 are at risk and get a recovery plan within 3 business days, with an executive sponsor for strategic accounts. The target annual churn rate is below 10 percent. Renewal conversations start 90 days before contract end. Enterprise customers receive a quarterly business review. Customer satisfaction is tracked alongside churn as an early warning signal, and support responsiveness targets are part of the success scorecard.
