Spoonful Information Security Policy. All employees must sign in with single sign-on and multi-factor authentication (MFA). Shared accounts are prohibited. Access follows least privilege and is reviewed every quarter. Access for departing staff must be revoked within 24 hours. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and stored in the company password manager. API keys and secrets must never be committed to source control. Customer personal data is classified Restricted and must be encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher in transit. Company laptops need full-disk encryption and a screen lock after 5 minutes. Suspected security incidents, including clicked phishing emails, must be reported to security@spoonful.example within 1 hour. All staff complete security training yearly, and phishing simulations run every quarter.
